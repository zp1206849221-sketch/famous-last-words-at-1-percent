# Famous Last Words at 1 Percent

A make.fun contest entry: the funniest one-line farewell a phone could say as
its battery hits 1 percent. The line is written in the phone's own voice, with
the solemnity of a deathbed declaration — it ascends to the great lock screen
above, retires its alarms, and delivers the twist at the end: every
verification code its owner needs, for his bank, his email, and his 6 a.m.
flight, dies with it.

The entry line (172 characters):

> At 1%, I ascend to the great lock screen above: tell my alarms they are retired, and tell my owner the codes for his bank, his email, and his 6 a.m. flight all die with me.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt` (creating the `/output`
directory if it does not exist yet).
